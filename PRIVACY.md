# Privacy Policy – Game Review Radar

_Last updated: October 2026_

Game Review Radar is a free, non-profit community tool for analyzing public Google Play reviews.

## What we collect
- **Google sign-in:** your email address and name, used only to sign you in and show your account.
- **API keys (optional):** if you tick "Remember", your Gemini/Groq key is stored **encrypted**
  (Fernet/AES). It is decrypted only on the server to run requests you start. Otherwise the key is kept
  only for your browser session.
- **Your settings,** saved comparisons and custom topic lists.
- **An activity log** (e.g. "added a game") used to enforce fair daily limits and to fix problems.
- **Messages** you send through the contact form.

## What we don't do
- We don't sell or share your data, show ads or track you across other sites.
- We don't store store reviewers' names – only the public text, rating, date and app version.

## Shared content
Games you add and the AI labels of their reviews become part of the shared catalog that every visitor
can see. Your name appears next to them **only** if you turn on "Show me on the Community page".

## Your choices
You can remove saved keys at any time, and delete your account in **Settings → Privacy & account**.
This deletes your keys, settings, comparisons and topic lists. Games you added stay in the catalog
without your name.

## Services we use
Google (sign-in, Gemini API with your key), Supabase (database hosting), Streamlit Community Cloud
(app hosting), GitHub (code and weekly refresh job).

## Contact
Use the contact form on the app's **About & Community** page, or open an issue at
https://github.com/bugracamci/game-review-analyzer.
